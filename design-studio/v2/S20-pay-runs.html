Tess’s home: approved bills in, correct payments out

The check processor turns approved bills into payments: queued, numbered, printed or exported, positive-pay filed, released by someone else, cleared. Her home is the queue across clients. Inside a client, Pay runs is one page with six tabs, and the five steps of a run sit above every tab.

The dialog that replaces window.confirm

One reason from a short list, a note for the audit trail, and a ledger of exactly what changes. The destructive verb names the check and the reissue number, and the toast repeats it.

Every number accounted for

The strip is the signature of this screen: cleared, outstanding, void, this run, next on stock. An auditor’s gap test, drawn. 28 cleared, 8 outstanding, 2 void before today.

A void is a reason

The dialog names what changes: the void, the reissue number, and the positive-pay file. No GL entry because 10524 was never issued.
