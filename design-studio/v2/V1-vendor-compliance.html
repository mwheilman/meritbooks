Read a vendor in one glance

Nine cells in a fixed order. Solid is met, a 45° hatch is expiring, a slash is not met, a dot waits for review. Words are in the panel and in screen-reader text, never colour alone.

The limit, not just the flag

A short limit shows as a bar against the required tick: 500,000 on the certificate, 1,000,000 required. The agent gets the same sentence.

Holds stop money, not work

Bills still approve and post; the liability is real. A held bill drops out of Thursday’s run with its reason, and only an accounting manager can grant an exception.

Nobody types a reminder

Every row’s chase is already running: welcome, correction, renewal at T-30 / T-15 / T-0. Buttons here are nudges and previews, not the engine.

Contracts become rules

Drop the insurance exhibit from a subcontract; each requirement is proposed with its source sentence, the same pattern as the expense policy (W2).

The agent is a recipient

Certificates are issued by agents. Copying the agent on COI requests is what gets them right the first time.

Quiet, then firm

Quiet hours and weekends hold texts. Seven days of grace after expiry, then the hold, then escalation to the client.
