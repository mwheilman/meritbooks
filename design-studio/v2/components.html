Brand hexes are exact. Ramps are sampled from the guide’s Usage page; nothing else enters the palette. Functional colours are dark enough to sit beside Midnight and Steel.

09//Evidence

Notes to the figures

The AI never appears as a chat bubble or a sparkle. It appears as an auditor’s footnote: a claim, the evidence as a dotted-leader ledger that sums, its basis and confidence, and the next action. On owner screens notes live in a Canvas margin. In a work panel the same note sits inline on Sky.

  • .nref on the figure ⇄ .note[data-note]
  • .ev evidence · .ev-total must equal the claim
  • .why for reasons for and .against
