One settings home: Merit’s, each client’s, and yours

Today’s Settings is one page of tabs that mixes firm-wide and per-client choices. Here every area has one home in one of three scopes, side by side: 13 for the firm, 14 for whichever client is picked, 7 for you. Client areas that have a firm default say whether they follow it or override it.

Three scopes, no hunting

Firm, client, you. An area lives in exactly one, and the word at the top of the column says who it applies to.

Overrides are visible

A client area with a Merit default carries a mark: follows, or overrides with a count. Areas that are naturally per-client say so.

Status, not just links

Each row carries one phrase from the area itself: 1 card not mapped, MFA 12 of 15. Three amber rows are the work.

The move is explained

For one release a banner and “Where did it go?” map every old tab. Old URLs open the new area.
