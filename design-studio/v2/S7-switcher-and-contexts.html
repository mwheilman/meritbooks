Order

Workspace first, then the last five recents, then Pinned, My clients, All clients (only with all-client scope), Consolidated (only groups you hold). The Platform section exists for platform staff only.

Every row says how it’s going

Close-status pip plus open-item count, so the switcher is also a triage list. Own books carry their tag here too, before you switch.

Keeps your place

Switch from Bank feed and you land on the new client’s Bank feed. If that page doesn’t exist there (say, in a consolidated view), you land on its home.

Never authority

The chosen context is a hint. The server checks access on every request. A group row only appears when you can open every member.

S7//Decision record

Why not amber, steel and red bands

IA-SPEC §3.1 asked for a 4px amber, steel or red band under the bar. Amber isn’t in the Merit palette, a 4px line goes unnoticed after a week, and amber reads as “something is wrong” when posting to the firm’s own books is legitimate work. Each state now changes the bar itself and carries one mark that means something.
