Export from Innergy in 4 steps

Recipe v1 · draft, confirm with ICC’s Innergy admin
  1. 1
    Open Innergy and go to the Jobs list

    Filter status to Active and On hold. Leave the date range blank so every open job comes through.

  2. 2
    Export the WIP view to Excel

    Use the WIP columns: contract, change orders, cost to date, billed to date, retainage. Keep the header row.

  3. 3
    Export open AR and AP

    Both as Excel, as of Sep 30. These enrich QuickBooks’ agings; they don’t replace them.

  4. 4
    Drop the three files below

    We recognize Innergy exports by their headers. Rename nothing.

Two honest paths

Export & drop for the opening (it carries its own totals, so it certifies). A QuickBooks bridge for daily activity, tagged Temporary with an end condition.

Recipes are steps

Numbered because order matters. Each step names the screen and the columns. Version and draft status are printed on the recipe.

Files prove themselves

The dropped WIP file is recognized by its headers and tied to the GL before mapping: the 11,480.00 gap is the one on the Tower.

The upgrade path

When Innergy grants access, the button changes from Export to Connect. Staging, mappings and tie-out stay as they are.
