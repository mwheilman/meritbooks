Consent, scoped

Reason, ticket, clients and duration are all required, and the tenant sees them verbatim. Read-only is the default; read & write is its own request.

The danger colour, once

Brick marks consented access everywhere it appears: the request card’s rule, the open session in the log, and the support band in S7. Nothing else on these screens uses it as a frame.

It ends by itself

The clock runs from approval, not from the request. Either side can end it early; expiry is logged like any other event.

No figures without a grant

The platform form shows the tenant’s name, type, client count and admin. Nothing from the ledger appears until the session opens.
