Jordan’s policy: from a Word document to rules people acknowledge

Heartland wrote its travel and expense policy in Word. Jordan drops it; the compiler proposes rules in the fixed schema, each tied to the sentence it came from; anything the schema can’t express is kept word for word and never enforced. A 90-day simulation shows what would change before anyone is affected. Publishing schedules the version and asks every employee in scope to acknowledge it on their phone (W1).

Heartland Heating & Cooling · Travel and Expense Policy · v3 draftPage 2 of 6

4. Receipts and documentation

1Receipts are required for every expense of $25.00 or more. U1Client meals must list the names and companies of everyone attending.

5. Meals and lodging

2Meals while traveling overnight are limited to $75 per day. 3Lodging may not exceed $159 per night in Iowa U2and $229 per night outside Iowa unless approved in advance. 5Employees traveling overnight receive a per diem at GSA rates for the destination city.

6. Travel

4Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at 70 cents per mile.

7. Alcohol, gifts and entertainment

6Alcohol is not reimbursable except at client entertainment, up to $50 per event. 7Gifts to customers over $25 require pre-approval. 8Fines, traffic tickets and personal entertainment are never reimbursable.

8. Approvals and purchasing

9Expenses up to $500 are approved by the supervisor, up to $2,500 by the department head, and above that by the controller. U3Tools and equipment over $500 must be bought through a purchase order, not on a company card. U4Expense reports are due within 30 days of the expense.

Config, not code

The compiler can only fill the fixed ruleset schema: caps in cents, severities, tiers. It never writes logic, so a misread is a wrong number you can see, not a behaviour you can’t.

Every rule has a source

The number on a rule is the number on its sentence. Reviewing is reading the two side by side, not trusting a summary.

What it can’t do, it says

Four clauses have no home in the schema yet. They stay as guidance, listed, so nobody assumes the system enforces them.

AI paused?

The same review screen opens empty with the document beside it. Jordan types the nine values; everything after is deterministic.
