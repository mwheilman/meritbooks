Create · consolidated kind · 9 members from ownership · Swan Creek joins from its acquisition date.Tree from entity_ownership · method per edge
Two kinds, one line each

Consolidated follows ownership and applies %, equity method and minority interest. Combined is for sister clients: summed, intercompany removed, nothing owned.

Ownership is read, not typed

The tree is the legal ownership record, effective-dated. Changing a % happens on the client’s legal tab, so the group and the books can’t disagree.

Eliminations are lines

Seven fee pairs, each shown, summing to the one total. Nothing is netted silently; the equity-method client is named for why it isn’t there.

Who isn’t in it, and why

Five clients have no ownership link. They’re listed, with the way to report them together: a combined group.
