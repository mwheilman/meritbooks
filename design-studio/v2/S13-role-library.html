Three owners, one list

Platform templates are the source; Merit owns tenant roles; a client owns its client roles. The chip and the line under it say which.

Holders are a link

“3 people · 13 clients” opens the people, so the cost of a change is one click from the role.

Updates are a dot, not a takeover

A blue dot under status means a newer template exists. Nothing changes until someone adopts it.

Archive explains itself

The row menu shows Archive locked with the reason: a role with holders can’t be archived.

Template updates

Adopt all, adopt safe changes, pick, or dismiss. Nothing applies by itself.
