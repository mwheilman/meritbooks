Two states, in words

Every role is what its holders have now. If someone is editing it, the Changes column says “Proposed changes · 4” and who’s drafting.

People are a link

“3 people · 13 clients” opens them, so the cost of a change is one click from the role.

Suggestions are optional

MeritBooks’ suggestions are one quiet, dismissible note. Nothing changes unless someone adds them.

Archive explains itself

The row menu shows Archive locked with the reason: a role people hold can’t be archived. History is one item in the same menu.
