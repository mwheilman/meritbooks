The bridge is the headline

Budget and actual are Midnight anchors; favourable steps are Electric; unfavourable steps wear the brick slash. Each step is focusable and drills.

Variance means effect on profit

Cost lines flip sign so every variance reads the same way: positive helps EBITDA, parentheses hurt it. F/U never relies on colour alone.

Commentary is a ledger, not prose

Each note states a claim, lists evidence that sums to it, names its basis, and offers the next action. It stays a draft until reviewed.

Department owners are named

Each section carries its owner, so “Ask Brent” sends a request to the person who can answer, attached to the line.

Flexed, not static

Comparing cost to a budget flexed to actual revenue separates “we sold less” from “it cost more to deliver”. That’s the difference between the volume and margin steps.

Down to the bill

Accounts → entries → bill → job. The bill opens in its own drawer with the source PDF; nothing leaves the ledger.

Explain this variance

The cause comes from job cost lines matched to vendor price lists. The words are AI; the numbers and the matching are deterministic.

Columns make room

With the panel open, the comparison columns fold away instead of squeezing every figure.
