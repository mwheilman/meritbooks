Distance, not a pass mark

A green tick hides how close you are. The ruler puts the limit, today and the next three quarters on one line; the hatched end is breach.

Forecast from the plan

Forecast marks come from reforecast v3 in S23, so changing a driver moves the covenant. The Carrier scenario is named where it would breach.

Definitions are the bank’s

Each test is computed the way the agreement defines it, parsed from the loan documents and confirmed by the controller. Van notes are excluded because §6.2 says so.

Preliminary says so

Until September hard-closes, the figures are marked preliminary and the certificate won’t use them.
