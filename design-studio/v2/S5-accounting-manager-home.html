What gets measured
Open state · 1440 wide · Jordan’s scope is 13 clients; Merit Management Group LLC (own books) is kept by the controller and isn’t in it.Close grid 12 × 4 · rows 38px · capacity scale 0–50h
The staircase

Each step is a cell: green with a tick when done, outlined where the work is, red-outlined where it’s stuck. Sorted by distance from close, the done cells march right as you read down.

Status says why

Every off-track row carries its reason beside the word: the feed, the unanswered request, the person who is over. Numbers are open items; the totals row re-sums whatever the filter shows.

Move people where the work is

Capacity is drawn in hours on one scale, so 45 against 35 is visible from across the room. Drag a client block to a person; the confirm states both loads and any access it grants.

Below the fold, still work

Blocked work has a fix on every row; stale requests escalate in one press. Team quality shows an honest empty state until there’s a closed month to measure.
