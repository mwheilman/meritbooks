Proof is the product

Three proofs, in order. The tie-out grid puts every month of every account at zero against the source. The shadow close runs a real month in both systems and explains every difference. Together they become a Conversion Certificate: the preparer signs, a different person approves, and the client’s pre-cutover months close for good.
