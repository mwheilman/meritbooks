</div> <div class='o' aria-hidden='true'>A</div> <h1>The Annual<br>Report</h1> <p class='lead'>
<b>MeritBooks should read like the most rigorous financial document an owner has ever held — and then answer back.</b> We took the 2026 guide literally: Syne headlines in Electric Blue, 
thin blue rules, 
outlined numerals, 
the “02 // section” labels, 
and full-bleed Midnight chapter breaks. 
Then we held every table to annual-report standards. 
Figures are right-aligned and tabular, 
negatives sit in parentheses with a hanging paren so digits stay aligned, 
a single rule marks subtotals, 
and a blue double rule closes each total. 
Owner screens stay calm and editorial. 
The bookkeeper’s screen is dense and keyboard-first.</p> <div class='sig'>
<span class='eyebrow'>Signature · Notes to the figures</span> In a printed report, 
any number that needs explaining carries a note reference.<sup>1</sup> We made that the AI’s voice. 
<b>Every figure the AI touched carries a blue note number. 
The note sits in the margin and gives the claim, 
the evidence as a dotted-leader ledger, 
its confidence and basis, 
and the next action.</b> Hover either end and both light up. 
The bank-feed detail panel is itself a note. 
The AI never shows up as a chat bubble or a sparkle; 
it shows up as an auditor’s footnote you can check.</div> <nav class='toc' aria-label='Screens'> <a href='portfolio.html'>
