## Part I — Orientation & ground rules
Three pillars: (1) **book of record / the GL** — MeritBooks OWNS it, NOT an automation layer on QBO/Sage ("the defining architectural fact"); (2) AI automation eliminating manual entry (AP/receipts from monitored email → AI extracts → proposes for approval); (3) native FP&A. **Generic platform** — Merit is a standard tenant; never hardcode Merit-specifics. **Module 1 of 12**; time tracking in the separate PM module; **MeritContext out of scope.** AI gateway is Core-owned; tenant monthly budget binding across combined suite usage. Hard rules: RLS `org_id=get_org_id()` never `auth.uid()`; `export const dynamic="force-dynamic"`; typecheck from `apps/web/`; tabula rasa (only COA pre-seeded, per-tenant). **Stripe conventions (I.5a):** keys in Vercel env not Vault; fetch HTTP client; destination-charge `payment_intent.*` fire on the platform account → webhook listens there; `PLATFORM_ORG_ID` no-op if unset. Feature depth standard (Rules 13–16): "complete" means meeting the module's FPB.

## Part II — Canonical model (Session 12 pivot)
**Company → Department → Job.** Departments user-defined, each own P&L/budget/FP&A. Internal charges are **invoice-based, not hours-based**, and eliminate at consolidation: revenue method DR *Interdepartmental Services Cost (receiver)* / CR *Interdepartmental Services Revenue (provider)* (both `is_eliminating=true`); cost-transfer method DR/CR receiver/provider cost account. **The providing department's effective method governs.** AI JE & categorization = one engine, two front doors (NL composer + inbound categorizer) → proposed balanced JE through one review+audit path. **Guardrails (non-negotiable):** clarify before booking (ask ONE disambiguating question when ambiguous, don't pattern-match); advisory only (human approves; auto-post OFF); non-standard GAAP flagged with documented reason; full audit on every AI action. Seven-stage pipeline; auto-post OFF by default. **II.7 Onboarding wizard (12 steps 0–11):** interview → org → companies → departments → **COA (source choice: standard/bring-your-own/hybrid)** → **Opening Balances/Conversion (the critical step; TB must balance)** → Plaid → M365 → vendors/customers → policies → team/roles → review & go-live. AI never enters credentials.

## Part VIII — Standing business logic
Rev-rec (per company; 9 resolution variants; COGS routing trees; monthly 1st @ 2 AM). Bank-feed matching (vendor 40 + amount 40 + date 20; >90/70–90/<70; AI ≥85/50–84/<50; auto-post OFF). Receipt chase (push+SMS; escalate 3/5/10; quiet 9pm–6am; thresholds $10/$25/$50/$100). Vendor compliance (W-9/COI/WC; missing→auto payment hold; chase every 2 weeks from 60 days before expiry; magic-link upload; Claude parses; override one-time/temporary/permanent, audit-logged). Month-end close 3 phases + Period Close Gate (bank recs, checklist, working papers, open items, compliance, intercompany — all pass; manager override needs reason). Inter-department charges (eliminating 4990/5990/5991; provider's method governs). **AI governance (VIII.7):** advisory by default; SoD on the AI itself ("the same agent must never both originate and approve a payment or non-routine/material entry... you only get to lose a controller's trust once"); everything explainable & logged; per-task autonomy dial; "there is never a global 'let the AI run' switch."
